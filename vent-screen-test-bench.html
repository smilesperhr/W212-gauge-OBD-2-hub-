Drive simulation

All three boards use the same 320 × 240 ILI9341 panel over SPI, so they refresh at the same speed. The 2.8 in just has bigger pixels. Frame rates shift with what each screen is drawing. The drive loop launches through gears 1 to 3 with a 6,200 rpm shift point on the 7G-Tronic ratios, then coasts back to idle. Numbers are modelled, so treat them as close estimates, not measurements.
