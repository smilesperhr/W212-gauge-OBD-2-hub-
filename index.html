2011 MERCEDES E350 · W212 · M272

W212 Gauge Project

A custom ESP32 gauge and diagnostic display for a 2011 Mercedes E350. A footwell module reads the car over CAN through the OBD2 port and drives two dash screens: a pixel-art speed and RPM gauge, and a diagnostic screen that explains check engine codes. These pages are the design references and test benches the firmware is built to match.
